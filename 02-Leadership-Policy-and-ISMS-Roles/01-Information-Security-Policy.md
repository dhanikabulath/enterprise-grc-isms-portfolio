# Information Security Policy

## Purpose

This policy sets out Stark Industries Inc.'s approach to protecting its information, systems, and SaaS platform.

The Information Security Management System (ISMS) is based on ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and is used to manage information security risks in a consistent way.

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, systems, and services within the defined ISMS scope.

It covers the systems and activities used to develop, operate, and support Stark Industries' cloud-based SaaS marketing platform.

## Information Security Principles

Stark Industries aims to protect:

- **Confidentiality** – information should only be accessible to authorised people;
- **Integrity** – information and systems should remain accurate and protected from unauthorised changes; and
- **Availability** – systems and information should be available when they are needed.

## Information Security Objectives

The company's overall security priorities are to:

- securely develop and operate the SaaS platform;
- maintain system availability and data integrity;
- reduce the risk of unauthorised access;
- improve security awareness among employees; and
- maintain an effective ISMS aligned with ISO/IEC 27001:2022.

Detailed and measurable information security objectives are maintained separately as part of the ISMS.

## Risk Management

Information security decisions should be based on risk.

Stark Industries identifies information assets, assesses threats and vulnerabilities, evaluates risks, and selects appropriate controls where treatment is required.

Risks and controls should be reviewed when significant changes occur to the business or technology environment.

## Responsibilities

The CEO has overall accountability for information security and the ISMS.

The Information Security Manager is responsible for developing, implementing, and maintaining the ISMS.

Employees and contractors are responsible for following the information security policies and procedures that apply to their work.

## Compliance

Stark Industries maintains its ISMS in line with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and applicable contractual requirements.

Changes to legal, regulatory, or contractual requirements should be reviewed to determine whether they affect the ISMS.

## Continual Improvement

Stark Industries is committed to improving the ISMS over time.

This includes:

- reviewing information security risks;
- monitoring the effectiveness of controls;
- reviewing security incidents;
- performing internal audits;
- conducting management reviews; and
- addressing identified nonconformities.

Lessons from these activities should be used to improve security controls and ISMS processes.

## Leadership Commitment

Senior management supports the implementation and continual improvement of the ISMS.

Management is responsible for making sure information security supports the company's business objectives, appropriate resources are available, and security responsibilities are understood across the organisation.

The effectiveness of the ISMS is reviewed through defined monitoring, audit, and management review activities.

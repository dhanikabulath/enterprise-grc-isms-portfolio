# Risk Treatment Process

## Purpose

This document explains how Stark Industries Inc. treats information security risks identified through the ISMS risk assessment process.

The aim is to reduce unacceptable risks to a level that the company can reasonably accept and manage.

## Scope

The risk treatment process applies to information security risks identified within the ISMS scope that require further action.

## Treatment Options

Stark Industries uses four risk treatment options.

### Mitigate

Apply security controls to reduce the likelihood or impact of the risk.

### Avoid

Stop or change the activity that creates the risk.

### Transfer

Transfer part of the risk to another party, for example through a contract, supplier arrangement, or insurance.

### Accept

Accept the risk when it is within the organisation's risk tolerance or where further treatment is not considered proportionate.

## Control Selection

Controls are selected based on:

- the severity of the risk;
- the affected asset and threat;
- the business context;
- available resources; and
- relevant ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A controls.

Custom security measures may also be used where appropriate.

Controls selected through the treatment process are recorded and justified in the Statement of Applicability.

## Risk Treatment Plan

Each treatment entry records:

- Risk ID;
- affected asset;
- risk description;
- risk level before treatment;
- selected treatment option;
- selected controls;
- residual risk;
- responsible person;
- target completion date; and
- implementation status.

## Residual Risk

Once controls have been implemented, the risk is reassessed.

If the remaining risk is acceptable, no additional treatment is required.

If the residual risk remains above the accepted threshold, it must either receive further treatment or be formally accepted by top management.

## Responsibilities

### Information Security Manager

Oversees the risk treatment process and validates whether implemented controls are effective.

### Cyber Security GRC Analyst

Maintains the Risk Treatment Plan and coordinates updates to treatment records.

### Risk Owners

Make sure assigned controls and treatment actions are implemented.

### CEO / Top Management

Approves the formal acceptance of residual risk when required.

## Monitoring and Review

Risk treatment plans are reviewed monthly by the Information Security Manager.

Treatment progress may also be reviewed during internal audits and management reviews.

Control effectiveness can be checked through technical testing, security monitoring, user feedback, and other available evidence.

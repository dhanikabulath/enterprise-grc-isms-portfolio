# Acceptable Use Policy

**Organisation:** Stark Industries Inc.  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Owner:** Information Security Manager  
**Status:** Final  
**Review Frequency:** Annual  

## Purpose

This policy defines how Stark Industries personnel and authorised third parties are expected to use company systems, devices, services, and information.

The aim is to protect company assets from misuse and reduce the risk of unauthorised access, data loss, malware, and other security incidents.

## Scope

This policy applies to employees, contractors, interns, and third parties who access Stark Industries systems or information.

It covers:

- company laptops and other devices;
- networks and cloud services;
- email and communication tools;
- SaaS platforms;
- company applications; and
- company information and data.

## Acceptable Use

Users must:

- use company systems and information only for authorised business purposes;
- follow password and MFA requirements;
- lock workstations when left unattended;
- protect sensitive information during storage and transmission;
- access only systems and information they are authorised to use;
- report suspected security incidents or policy violations promptly; and
- complete required information security awareness training.

## Unacceptable Use

Users must not:

- share credentials or use another person's account;
- install unauthorised software or applications;
- bypass security controls or access restrictions;
- use company resources for illegal or unethical activities;
- access or distribute inappropriate or pirated material;
- transfer company information to unauthorised personal storage;
- use company systems for unauthorised personal commercial activities; or
- deliberately introduce malware or other malicious software.

## Protection of Company Information

Users are responsible for handling company information according to its classification.

Sensitive information must not be copied, shared, uploaded, or transferred to unauthorised locations or services.

Where required, sensitive information must be encrypted in transit and at rest.

## Access and Authentication

Users must protect their authentication credentials and must not disclose passwords, MFA codes, or other authentication information.

Access to company systems and information must be limited to authorised business requirements.

Suspected credential compromise must be reported immediately.

## Security Incidents

Users must promptly report suspected:

- phishing;
- malware;
- unauthorised access;
- lost or stolen devices;
- accidental disclosure of company information; and
- other suspected security incidents.

Users must cooperate with security investigations where required.

## User Responsibilities

All users are responsible for understanding and following this policy.

Failure to comply may result in access restrictions, disciplinary action, or other appropriate action depending on the nature of the violation.

## Review

This policy is reviewed annually and when significant changes occur to Stark Industries systems, business operations, security requirements, or regulatory obligations.

## ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Alignment

This policy supports the following Annex A controls:

- A.5.10 — Acceptable use of information and other associated assets
- A.5.15 — Access control
- A.5.16 — Identity management

# Supplier and Vendor Management Policy

**Organisation:** Stark Industries Inc.  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Owner:** Information Security Manager  
**Status:** Final  
**Review Frequency:** Annual  

## Purpose

This policy defines how Stark Industries evaluates and manages security risks associated with suppliers, vendors, and service providers.

## Scope

This policy applies to suppliers that may affect the ISMS, including:

- AWS;
- GitHub;
- SaaS providers;
- development partners;
- managed service providers; and
- consultants.

## Supplier Classification

Suppliers are classified according to risk.

### Critical

Suppliers with access to production systems or sensitive information.

Example: AWS.

### Operational

Suppliers that support internal systems or workflows.

Example: Jira.

### Low Risk

Suppliers without access to company systems or sensitive information.

## Supplier Risk Assessment

Critical and operational suppliers must undergo a risk assessment before onboarding.

Assessment should consider:

- security certifications such as ISO 27001 or SOC 2;
- data-handling practices;
- service availability and SLAs; and
- legal or compliance requirements.

## Contractual Requirements

Supplier agreements must include appropriate requirements for:

- confidentiality;
- data protection;
- breach notification;
- evidence of security controls or audit rights; and
- service availability, support, and patching.

## Monitoring

Critical suppliers are reviewed at least annually.

Reviews may include:

- updated security reports or certifications;
- SLA and performance review; and
- security incidents or breach history.

## Supplier Offboarding

When a supplier relationship ends:

- access to Stark Industries systems must be revoked;
- company information must be returned or securely destroyed; and
- final verification or audit may be performed where required.

## Roles and Responsibilities

### Procurement / Legal

Ensures supplier contracts contain appropriate clauses.

### Information Security Manager

Classifies suppliers, reviews risks, and supports supplier audits.

### System Owners

Request onboarding and monitor supplier performance.

## Exceptions

Exceptions must be documented, approved by the Information Security Manager, and mitigated through alternative controls where possible.

## Policy Review

This policy is reviewed annually and when significant environmental or regulatory changes occur.

# Asset Management and Data Classification Policy

**Organisation:** Stark Industries Inc.  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Owner:** Information Security Manager  
**Status:** Final  
**Review Frequency:** Annual  

## Purpose

This policy defines how Stark Industries identifies, manages, owns, and classifies information assets throughout their lifecycle.

The purpose is to make sure assets receive appropriate protection based on their value, sensitivity, and security requirements.

## Scope

This policy applies to all information assets within the Stark Industries ISMS, including:

- physical devices;
- software;
- cloud environments;
- company data;
- applications; and
- personnel with access to company systems.

## Objectives

Stark Industries aims to:

- maintain an inventory of information assets;
- assign ownership to each asset;
- classify assets according to their sensitivity;
- protect assets according to their value and risk; and
- prevent unauthorised access, disclosure, modification, or destruction.

## Asset Types

Assets covered by the ISMS include:

### Information Assets

Examples include:

- source code;
- company and application data; and
- documentation.

### IT Assets

Examples include:

- servers;
- employee laptops;
- CI/CD tools; and
- GitHub.

### Cloud Services

This includes AWS infrastructure supporting Stark Industries' systems and services.

### Applications

Examples include:

- internal administration tools; and
- the SaaS marketing platform.

### Personnel and Roles

Personnel are also considered where their access rights and responsibilities affect information security.

## Asset Inventory

All assets must be recorded in the Stark Industries Asset Inventory.

The Cyber Security GRC Analyst is responsible for maintaining the inventory.

Each asset record should include:

- Asset ID;
- description;
- asset type;
- location;
- owner;
- classification; and
- associated threats and vulnerabilities.

The inventory is reviewed at least annually and when significant changes occur.

## Asset Ownership

Each asset must have an assigned owner.

Asset owners are responsible for:

- making sure the asset is appropriately protected;
- assigning the correct classification;
- managing relevant changes; and
- reporting risks affecting the asset.

## Acceptable Use

Users must follow the Stark Industries Acceptable Use Policy when using or handling company assets.

Misuse, negligence, or unauthorised use may result in disciplinary action.

Lost or compromised assets must be reported immediately.

## Asset Classification

Assets are classified according to their confidentiality, integrity, and availability requirements.

Stark Industries uses four classification levels.

### Confidential

Restricted to authorised personnel only.

### Restricted

Limited access for operational use.

### Internal Use Only

Available for general employee use but not intended for public release.

### Public

Approved for public release.

## Information Labelling

Digital and physical information must be labelled according to its classification.

Classification labels should remain with the information throughout its lifecycle.

## Media Handling

Storage devices containing sensitive information must be appropriately protected.

Requirements include:

- encrypting sensitive information;
- tracking storage devices when they are moved;
- wiping devices before disposal or reuse; and
- using secure erasure methods when disposing of information.

## Asset Lifecycle

Assets must be tracked from acquisition through use to decommissioning.

When an asset is decommissioned, it must be securely disposed of and appropriate records retained.

## Roles and Responsibilities

### Asset Owners

Responsible for asset registration, classification, and protection.

### Cyber Security GRC Analyst

Maintains the Asset Inventory, supports audit activities, and tracks asset-related risks.

### Information Security Manager

Oversees the classification process and coordinates policy reviews.

### All Employees

Responsible for following requirements for the handling, reporting, and use of company assets.

## Policy Review

This policy is reviewed annually and when significant changes occur to the environment or regulatory requirements.

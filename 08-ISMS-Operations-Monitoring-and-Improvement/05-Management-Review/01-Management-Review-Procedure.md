# Management Review Procedure

**Organisation:** Stark Industries Inc.  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Owner:** Information Security Manager  
**Status:** Final  
**Review Frequency:** Annual  

## Purpose

This procedure defines how Stark Industries conducts management reviews of the Information Security Management System.

The review is used to confirm that the ISMS remains suitable, adequate, effective, and aligned with business and compliance requirements.

## Scope

The management review covers the full ISMS scope, including:

- cloud infrastructure;
- SaaS platform development;
- internal tools;
- supporting business and security processes; and
- third-party management.

## Objectives

Management reviews are conducted to:

- confirm alignment between the ISMS and business strategy;
- consider the organisation's risk tolerance;
- review progress against information security objectives;
- identify opportunities for continual improvement; and
- address nonconformities and significant internal or external changes.

## Management Review Inputs

Each review considers:

- internal audit results;
- feedback from customers, partners, and other relevant stakeholders;
- security incidents and breaches;
- identified nonconformities;
- monitoring and measurement results;
- KPI performance;
- backup performance;
- training completion;
- updates to risk assessments and risk treatment;
- status of corrective actions;
- significant changes to internal or external issues;
- progress against information security objectives; and
- resource requirements such as personnel, tools, and training.

## Management Review Outputs

The review should result in decisions or actions relating to:

- improvements to the ISMS;
- changes to the Risk Treatment Plan;
- changes to the Statement of Applicability where required;
- allocation of personnel, tools, training, or other resources;
- approval of revised policies or procedures; and
- identification of new risks and opportunities.

## Participants

### CEO

Provides overall ISMS oversight and makes strategic decisions.

### Information Security Manager

Coordinates the review and prepares relevant reports.

### Cyber Security GRC Analyst

Provides metrics, audit findings, and risk updates.

### IT / Infrastructure Engineer

Provides operational and infrastructure performance information.

### Security Analyst

Provides incident information and penetration-testing results.

## Management Review Records

The following records are retained:

- meeting agenda;
- attendance record;
- meeting minutes;
- decisions and action items;
- updated Risk Register where applicable;
- updated Statement of Applicability where applicable; and
- evidence of assigned actions and follow-up.

## Review and Updates

This procedure is reviewed annually and after major changes to the ISMS.

Additional management reviews may be conducted following significant changes, incidents, or audit results.

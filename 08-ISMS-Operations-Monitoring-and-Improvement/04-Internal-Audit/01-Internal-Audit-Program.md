# Internal Audit Program

**Organisation:** Stark Industries Inc.  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Owner:** Information Security Manager  
**Status:** Final  
**Review Frequency:** Annual  

## Purpose

This program defines how Stark Industries reviews the effectiveness and compliance of its Information Security Management System.

The audit process is intended to confirm that the ISMS is operating as expected and to identify nonconformities, risks, and opportunities for improvement.

## Scope

The internal audit covers the full ISMS scope, including:

- AWS cloud platforms;
- SaaS platform development and operations;
- internal tools and supporting infrastructure;
- information security policies and procedures;
- security training; and
- supplier and third-party management.

## Audit Objectives

Internal audits are used to:

- verify conformity with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 requirements;
- check compliance with Stark Industries policies and procedures;
- identify nonconformities and improvement opportunities;
- confirm progress against ISMS objectives; and
- assess whether controls recorded in the Statement of Applicability are operating effectively.

## Audit Criteria

Audits are performed against:

- ISO/IEC 27001:2022 requirements;
- Stark Industries ISMS policies and procedures; and
- legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations relevant to the SaaS platform.

## Audit Frequency

The full ISMS scope is audited annually.

Additional audits may be performed:

- after major changes to the ISMS or business operations;
- following significant security incidents or nonconformities; or
- where required by management review decisions.

## Audit Schedule

| Audit Period | Focus Area | Lead Auditor | Planned Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| Annual Audit | Full ISMS scope | Appointed Internal Auditor | To be scheduled |
| Additional Audit | Targeted areas such as incidents or high-risk controls | Appointed Internal Auditor | As required |

## Auditor Independence

Auditors must remain independent of the area being audited.

The Information Security Manager assigns auditors based on their competence and impartiality.

External resources may be used where additional expertise or independence is required.

## Audit Methods

Audit activities may include:

- review of policies, procedures, logs, and reports;
- interviews with relevant personnel;
- observation of processes and control implementation;
- sampling of records and evidence; and
- testing operational controls where appropriate.

## Audit Records

The following records are retained:

- audit plan and schedule;
- audit checklists and working papers;
- evidence collected during the audit;
- audit reports and findings;
- Corrective Action Requests; and
- follow-up records.

## Reporting and Follow-Up

Audit reports are issued to management within 14 days after completion of the audit.

Corrective actions are tracked according to their severity and assigned deadlines.

Audit results are included in the management review process.

## Review

This audit program is reviewed annually and after major changes to the ISMS.

Lessons learned from completed audits are used to improve future audit activities.

# ISMS Scope

## Purpose

This document defines the scope of the Information Security Management System (ISMS) for Stark Industries Inc. in line with ISO/IEC 27001:2022.

The scope sets the boundaries of the ISMS and identifies the systems, people, processes, and information assets that need to be considered when managing information security risks.

## Scope Statement

The ISMS covers the design, development, deployment, and maintenance of Stark Industries Inc.'s cloud-based SaaS marketing platform.

It includes the people, processes, systems, infrastructure, and information assets directly involved in delivering and supporting the service.

The scope includes:

- the SaaS marketing platform;
- production systems hosted on AWS;
- development environments;
- source code repositories;
- CI/CD pipelines;
- supporting internal tools;
- employee laptops;
- personnel involved in developing, operating, and supporting the platform; and
- relevant third-party services and vendors supporting the platform.

## Technology Environment

Stark Industries relies on a cloud-based technology environment to develop and operate its SaaS platform.

Important parts of the environment include:

- AWS cloud infrastructure;
- GitHub;
- source code repositories;
- CI/CD pipelines;
- employee laptops;
- SaaS applications; and
- supporting internal systems and tools.

These systems are considered when identifying information assets and assessing information security risks.

## Third-Party Services

Third-party services that support the operation or security of the SaaS platform are considered within the ISMS.

This includes cloud services such as AWS and GitHub, SaaS tools, development partners, managed service providers, and consultants where relevant.

Third-party risks are managed through supplier identification, risk assessment, contractual requirements, and ongoing monitoring.

## People

The ISMS applies to personnel who are responsible for or contribute to the security of systems and information within the defined scope.

This includes employees involved in:

- management;
- information security;
- IT and infrastructure;
- software development; and
- other activities supporting the SaaS platform.

Relevant contractors and third parties are also considered where their activities affect systems or information within the ISMS scope.

## Information Assets

Information assets within the scope include information and technology used to develop, operate, and support the SaaS platform.

Examples include:

- source code;
- company and application data;
- system documentation;
- AWS infrastructure;
- software applications;
- GitHub repositories;
- CI/CD tools; and
- employee laptops.

These assets will be recorded and assessed as part of the ISMS asset and risk management process.

## Scope Review

The ISMS scope should be reviewed at least annually and when significant changes affect the organisation, its systems, processes, threats, or third-party services.

Changes that affect the scope should also be considered as part of the risk assessment, internal audit, and management review processes.
